Introduction

From competitive flight to connected products.

Dario Neuenschwander is a Swiss product developer, entrepreneur and professional 3D RC helicopter pilot. He has been involved in aeromodelling since 2008, with experience spanning competitive flying, international demonstration flights, product development and technical work within the RC helicopter and FPV industries.

In 2017, he won the FAI Drone Racing World Cup after competing in a season that brought together 434 pilots from 37 countries. He is the founder and developer of DynoForce and co-founder of Tron Helicopters.

His professional work focuses on product development, mechanical engineering, electronics, embedded systems, sensor technology and force-measurement systems.

From 2009 to 2016, he worked as a test and development pilot for MSH, contributing to flight testing and development of the MSH Protos helicopter series and the MSH Brain flybarless system. He has been a SpinBlades Factory Pilot, Pro Team Pilot and R&D Contributor since 2012 and an official MKS Servo Team Pilot since 2014.

When XNOVA Motors was founded in 2014, he became its first test pilot and first team pilot. From 2016 to 2020, he additionally served as Team Manager for XNOVA Motors, and from 2016 to 2019 as Team Manager for Furious FPV.

RC Helicopter Career

Flying, testing and developing.

Dario Neuenschwander has been active in aeromodelling and professional 3D RC helicopter flying since 2008.

His career includes competition flying, international flight demonstrations, prototype testing, product development and technical collaboration with several internationally recognised RC helicopter manufacturers.

In 2010, he won a Swiss championship title in artistic RC model aerobatics to music. The award ceremony took place in Grenchen during the celebrations marking 100 years of aviation in Switzerland.

Produktentwicklung, Unternehmertum und Modellflug.

Dario Neuenschwander ist ein Schweizer Produktentwickler, Unternehmer und professioneller 3D-RC-Helikopter-Pilot sowie Gründer von DynoForce, Mitgründer von Tron Helicopters und Gewinner des FAI Drone Racing World Cup 2017.

2017 gewann er den FAI Drone Racing World Cup. Er ist Gründer und Entwickler von DynoForce sowie Mitgründer von Tron Helicopters. Seine berufliche Tätigkeit konzentriert sich auf Produktentwicklung, Mechanik, Elektronik, Embedded Systems, Sensorik und Kraftmesssysteme.

Von 2009 bis 2016 arbeitete er als Test- und Entwicklungspilot für MSH und war an der Flugerprobung und Entwicklung der MSH-Protos-Helikopterserie und des MSH Brain Flybarless-Systems beteiligt. Bei der Gründung von XNOVA Motors im Jahr 2014 wurde er der erste Testpilot und erste Team Pilot des Unternehmens. Von 2016 bis 2020 übernahm er zusätzlich die Funktion des XNOVA-Teammanagers; von 2016 bis 2019 war er ausserdem Team Manager bei Furious FPV.
