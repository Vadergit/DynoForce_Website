Introduction

From competitive flight to connected products.

Dario Neuenschwander is a Swiss product developer, entrepreneur and professional 3D RC helicopter pilot. He has been involved in aeromodelling since 2008, with experience spanning competitive flying, international demonstration flights, product development and technical work within the RC helicopter and FPV industries.

In 2017, he won the FAI Drone Racing World Cup after competing in a season that brought together 434 pilots from 37 countries. He is the founder and developer of DynoForce and co-founder of Tron Helicopters.

His professional work focuses on product development, mechanical engineering, electronics, embedded systems, sensor technology and force-measurement systems.

RC helicopter career

Flying, testing and developing.

Dario Neuenschwander has been involved in aeromodelling since 2008. His RC helicopter career includes competitive 3D flying, international demonstration flights, product testing and development work with manufacturers.

Official Tron Helicopters documentation describes him as a professional RC helicopter pilot and records his work on the MSH Protos helicopter series, the MSH Brain flybarless system and research and development for SpinBlades. MKS Servo also lists him as an RC helicopter team pilot.

His career also included team-management work for XNOVA Motors and Furious FPV, supporting international pilots and representing the brands within the RC helicopter and FPV racing communities. Exact public dates for these roles are still being documented.

Swiss championship title · historical category under verification

Artistic aerobatics to music.

In 2010, Dario Neuenschwander won a Swiss championship title in artistic RC model aerobatics performed to music. The award was presented in Grenchen during celebrations marking 100 years of aviation in Switzerland. The exact official competition category is currently being verified through historical records.

2017
FAI Drone Racing World Cup

World Cup winner.

The World Air Sports Federation recorded Dario Neuenschwander as the winner of the 2017 FAI Drone Racing World Cup. He finished first in a season with 434 pilots from 37 countries, scoring 142 points. An official FAI interview also records that he began drone racing two years before the title.

Produktentwicklung, Unternehmertum und Modellflug.

Dario Neuenschwander ist ein Schweizer Produktentwickler, Unternehmer und professioneller 3D-RC-Helikopter-Pilot. Er ist seit 2008 im Modellflug aktiv. Seine Laufbahn umfasst wettbewerbsmässiges Fliegen, internationale Showflüge, Entwicklungs- und Testarbeit sowie Teammanagement in der RC-Helikopter- und FPV-Branche.

2017 gewann er den FAI Drone Racing World Cup. Er ist Gründer und Entwickler von DynoForce sowie Mitgründer von Tron Helicopters. Seine berufliche Tätigkeit konzentriert sich auf Produktentwicklung, Mechanik, Elektronik, Embedded Systems, Sensorik und Kraftmesssysteme.

Im Jahr 2010 gewann er einen Schweizermeistertitel im künstlerischen RC-Modellflug zu Musik. Die genaue offizielle Wettbewerbskategorie wird derzeit anhand historischer Unterlagen überprüft und deshalb nicht als strukturierte Auszeichnung geführt.
