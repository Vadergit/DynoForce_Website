Biography

From competition to product development.

Dario Neuenschwander is a Swiss product developer and entrepreneur. He is the founder and developer of DynoForce, co-founder of Tron Helicopters, 2010 Swiss Champion in F6A Artistic Aerobatics and winner of the 2017 FAI Drone Racing World Cup. His work focuses on product development, mechanical engineering, electronics and performance measurement systems.

His technical background spans sensor technology, research and development, embedded systems and the design of connected hardware. At DynoForce, these disciplines come together in compact force-measurement devices and software for structured training and performance analysis.

2010
Swiss Championship · F6A Artistic Aerobatics

Swiss Champion.

In 2010, Dario Neuenschwander won the Swiss Championship in F6A Artistic Aerobatics, performing a 3D RC helicopter freestyle routine synchronised to music. The title was presented during the joint awards ceremony for the centenary celebration “100 Years of Aviation in Switzerland” in Grenchen.

2017
FAI Drone Racing World Cup

World Cup winner.

The World Air Sports Federation recorded Dario Neuenschwander as the winner of the 2017 FAI Drone Racing World Cup. He finished first among more than 430 pilots from 37 countries, with an overall score of 142 points.
